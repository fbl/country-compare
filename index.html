Assumptions per country

What each country model includes — and leaves out.

Estimates only. The tax rules are simplified models of 2026 rules. They were fact-checked in November 2026, mostly against secondary sources (accounting firms, tax portals), and some items could not be confirmed. Regional differences, pension-fund premiums, exit taxes when moving and currency moves are ignored. Living costs are scaled with Eurostat's national price levels for 2024, which include average rents but understate housing in capital cities for a newcomer. All company profit is paid out every year unless a Belgian company route is filled in. Use this to shortlist countries, then confirm with a local tax adviser before deciding anything.
